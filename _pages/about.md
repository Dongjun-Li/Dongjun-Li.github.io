I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in [Prof. Ziyou Song's research group](https://ziyou.engin.umich.edu/) at the [University of Michigan](https://umich.edu/), Ann Arbor. My research focuses on scientific machine learning, data-driven modeling and control, and predictive optimization for complex dynamical and energy systems.

My work aims to understand how data, partial physical knowledge, and structural relationships across dynamical systems can be jointly exploited for data-efficient, transferable, and reliable learning and decision-making. I also study data informativity, data-driven predictive control and optimization. These methodological developments are motivated by applications in electrified and intelligent transportation, including battery degradation-aware optimization, energy and thermal management, eco-driving, and connected and automated vehicles.

Before joining the University of Michigan, I received my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from the [National University of Singapore](https://www.nus.edu.sg/). I also received my B.E. and M.E. degrees in Automotive Engineering from [Chongqing University](https://www.cqu.edu.cn/).


## Research Interests

- Scientific machine learning and data-driven modeling of dynamical systems
- Structure-aware and transferable learning across dynamical systems
- Physics-informed and physics-guided learning
- Data informativity and data-driven predictive control
- Battery degradation-aware optimization and energy/thermal management
- Electrified transportation and energy systems
